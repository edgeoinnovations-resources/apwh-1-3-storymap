# Faith, Sea, and Rice

A scrolling storymap of South and Southeast Asia, c. 300 B.C.E. to 1431, built for AP World History: Modern, Topic 1.3 ("Developments in South and Southeast Asia, c. 1200–c. 1450").

**View it:** https://edgeoinnovations-resources.github.io/apwh-1-3-storymap/

## What's in it

- 18 chapters in chronological order. As you scroll, the map zooms to each region and shows it as it stood in c. 1250, 1335, 1360, or 1430.
- Audio clips from the Topic 1.3 chapter narration (`audio/`).
- Amber "Fact check" boxes that correct errors in the textbook and narration, with small numbered citations linked to a full bibliography at the end.

## Notes

- Borders are approximate and drawn for teaching.
- Corrections follow the "AMSCO 1.3 Fact-Check Review." Check monograph page numbers against a library copy before quoting them.
- Land and river outlines come from Natural Earth (public domain). The map uses d3 v7, loaded from cdnjs.
